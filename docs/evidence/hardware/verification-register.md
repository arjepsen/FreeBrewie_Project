# Hardware verification register

The [fact index](fact-index.md) contains 108 records: 23 verified and 85 disputed, with no proposed or provisionally accepted records. Five disputed audio records are non-blocking under an owner-approved removal; the remaining 80 disputed facts are material. The added runtime records resolve only their narrowly dated observations and do not establish physical identity, wiring, electrical limits, or clean-slate requirements.

## Verified facts

- [HW-001](facts/HW-001.md) verifies the bounded owner-observed marking claim that the fitted SOM reads `Olimex A13-SOM (512)`, PCB revision `G`. Olimex independently documents the A13-SOM-512 product and revision-G nomenclature; neither source is used to infer carrier wiring, the fitted RAM part, boot hardware, or electrical behavior.
- [HW-002](facts/HW-002.md) verifies that the fitted U1 marking explicitly reads `ATMEGA2560` and preserves all five owner-transcribed package lines. Microchip/Atmel ordering information independently confirms the model nomenclature; the remaining package codes are not decoded and no behavior, programming state, or board revision is inferred.
- [HW-003](facts/HW-003.md), [HW-015](facts/HW-015.md), and [HW-016](facts/HW-016.md) verify the manufacturer-published flash, SRAM, and EEPROM capacities of the fitted marked ATmega2560 model. Usable flash layout, runtime SRAM availability, and EEPROM contents, wear, protection, and allocation remain unresolved.
- [HW-094](facts/HW-094.md) verifies only the direct owner-observed carrier silkscreen strings `Brewie ARM board` and `V1p2`. No photograph or revision-controlled carrier artifact was inspected, and no population, connectivity, or electrical behavior is inferred.
- [HW-095](facts/HW-095.md) verifies that the fitted module's directly observed `Olimex A13-SOM (512)`, revision `G`, product marking denotes Olimex's 512 MB installed-DDR3 variant. It does not identify the exact DDR3 vendor, part, timings, runtime-usable memory, or memory health.
- [HW-071](facts/HW-071.md) verifies only that the audio-amplifier IC position is presently physically absent/unpopulated on the fitted `Brewie ARM board` revision `V1p2`. The owner's statement that the former chip was “fried” is attributed context, not a verified damage mechanism or root cause; no wider circuit or software behavior is inferred.
- [HW-096](facts/HW-096.md) and [HW-097](facts/HW-097.md) verify only the owner-reported dated inspection position and access state: the target machine is upside down on a table, providing access to the listed hardware. Neither record establishes original assembly, wiring, identity, mapping, or permanent access.
- [HW-098](facts/HW-098.md) verifies only that, at the time of the project owner's 2026-08-10 confirmation and physical observation, the target machine was unplugged from mains power. It does not establish electrical isolation beyond unplugged mains, discharge state, device mapping, or authority for probing or measurement.
- [HW-099](facts/HW-099.md) and [HW-100](facts/HW-100.md) verify only the owner-reported dated state that both physical heater loads and both physical pump loads are disconnected. They do not identify the loads, connectors, candidate endpoints, factory wiring, electrical limits, or later state.
- [HW-007](facts/HW-007.md) and [HW-008](facts/HW-008.md) verify that the fresh 2026-08-12 Olimex/Debian boot identified Goodix ID 911/version 1060 and enumerated it on Linux I2C bus 2 at address `0x14`. These runtime observations do not verify an exact fitted part, carrier routing, supply, pull-ups, IRQ/reset wiring, or electrical limits.
- [HW-101](facts/HW-101.md), [HW-102](facts/HW-102.md), [HW-103](facts/HW-103.md), and [HW-104](facts/HW-104.md) verify dated legacy ReBrewie runtime observations: kernel `Linux 3.4.90-Brewie #5`; community-edited Buildroot `2014.02-git-g3b4bd90-dirty`; the active `mmcblk0` partition sizes and EXT3 mounts; and PCF8563 detection on I2C0 at `0x51` as `rtc0`. These observations do not establish factory software, physical storage/RTC identity, wiring, or future requirements.
- [HW-105](facts/HW-105.md), [HW-106](facts/HW-106.md), and [HW-107](facts/HW-107.md) verify dated modern experimental runtime observations: kernel/architecture/model/Linux memory values; the A13 watchdog address and settings; and use of a fitted-on-date Realtek `0bda:8176` USB WLAN adapter with `rtl8192cu`. These observations do not select a future platform, watchdog policy, networking device, or driver.
- [HW-108](facts/HW-108.md) verifies that both fresh captures enumerate `ttyS0` and `ttyS1` at matching MMIO addresses. The captures corroborate Linux controller enumeration only and are not independent physical or configuration-lineage evidence; physical MCU connectivity, telemetry, electrical compatibility, and protocol remain unsupported.

## Owner-approved removals

- Exact fitted DDR3 package identity — including vendor, part number, speed grade, and timings — was removed from milestone scope by the project owner on 2026-08-10. The processor and DDR3 face the carrier and reading them would require separating fragile small connectors; the owner determined that connector-damage risk outweighs the value and explicitly prohibited requesting separation. Nominal installed capacity remains required and is independently verified as 512 MB in [HW-095](facts/HW-095.md). No exact DDR3 package or timing inference is permitted from revision alone.
- Buzzer/audio availability was removed from this milestone's required baseline by the project owner on 2026-08-10 because the buzzer is not needed in this design. [HW-071](facts/HW-071.md) still preserves the verified present physical absence of the carrier audio-amplifier IC; candidate audio-path facts [HW-068](facts/HW-068.md), [HW-069](facts/HW-069.md), [HW-070](facts/HW-070.md), [HW-079](facts/HW-079.md), and [HW-081](facts/HW-081.md) remain disputed historical evidence but no longer block milestone completion. The owner's statement that the former chip was “fried” is not treated as a verified damage mechanism and no software design is selected here.
